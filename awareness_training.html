1. Real vs. fake — can you spot the tell?

LEGITIMATE (example)
https://example-telecom.com/login
ET
Example Telecom – My Account
Official login page. Valid HTTPS certificate issued to the real company. No unsolicited email led here – user typed the address directly.
Mobile number
Password
  • Domain matches the company exactly
  • No urgency language
  • Reached by typing the address, not clicking a link
SUSPICIOUS (example)
https://exarnple-telec0m-secure.com.np/verify?uid=8f3a91&email=your.name@mail.com
ET
Urgent: Verify your account now
Email claimed the account would be suspended in 24 hours unless the user clicked to verify.
Full name
Date of birth
Password
  • Domain has extra letters and a wrong TLD (.com.np vs .com)
  • Letters swapped for lookalikes (rn instead of m, 0 instead of o)
  • Link contains a tracking ID and your email pre-filled
  • Creates urgency ("24 hours")
  • Asks for DOB – real login pages never need this
